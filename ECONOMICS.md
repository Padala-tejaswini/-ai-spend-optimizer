1. Value of a Converted Lead
Average Credex credit product purchase: $5,000

Gross margin on credit products: ~20% → $1,000 contribution per purchase

Lifetime value (LTV): assume 2 repeat purchases over 3 years → $2,000 per converted lead

Conclusion: A converted lead is worth ~$2,000 in gross margin to Credex.

3. Conversion Funnel Economics
We need to model:
Audit completed → Consultation booked → Credit purchase

Assume:

Audit → Consultation: 20%

Consultation → Purchase: 25%

Overall conversion: 0.20 × 0.25 = 5%

Example:

1,000 audits → 200 consultations → 50 purchases

50 purchases × $2,000 margin = $100,000 value

Break‑even check:

If average CAC per audit = $50, then 1,000 audits cost $50,000.

$100,000 margin – $50,000 CAC = $50,000 profit.

Funnel is profitable at ≥5% conversion.
